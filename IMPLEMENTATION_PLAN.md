# Suprhire Admin Implementation Plan

## 1. Admin Overview Dashboard

Add a single at-a-glance view of the product's most important operating and usage signals.

**The admin will see/use:**

- Users and companies
- Jobs and applications
- Resume imports and AI agents
- Interviews and calls
- Time-based trends and notable changes

---

## 2. User & Company Analytics

Add visibility into who is using Suprhire, how companies are participating, and where activity is concentrated.

**The admin will see/use:**

- User and company counts and growth
- Activity and engagement trends
- Users and usage by company
- Company-level jobs, applications, imports, and workflow activity

---

## 3. Product Usage Analytics

Add metrics that show which product capabilities are being used and how deeply customers engage with workflows.

**The admin will see/use:**

- Jobs posted and applications received
- Resume import activity
- Campaign and AI agent usage
- Interview and call engagement
- Feature adoption across companies

---

## 4. System Health & Readiness

Add early visibility into workflow reliability, operational health, and emerging bottlenecks.

**The admin will see/use:**

- Workflow attempts, successes, and failures
- Agent, import, interview, and call reliability
- Completion and drop-off rates
- Sudden degradation and bottleneck indicators

---

## 5. Abuse & Loophole Detection

Add signals and alerts that help identify suspicious activity, misuse, and possible exploitation of product workflows.

**The admin will see/use:**

- Unusual volume and rapid repeated actions
- Automated behavior signals
- Account and company risk indicators
- Alerts with examples such as an unusually large resume upload spike

---

## 6. Growth Analytics

Add a clear view of user, company, and product growth over time.

**The admin will see/use:**

- New users and companies by period
- Jobs, applications, imports, and feature adoption growth
- Interview and call usage trends
- Retention, expansion, and inactivity signals

---

## 7. Daily Admin Summary

Add a concise daily summary of what changed, what needs attention, and how the product is progressing.

**The admin will see/use:**

- Daily user, company, and product activity
- System health warnings
- Abuse and anomaly alerts
- Growth indicators and recommended action items

---

## 8. Shared Analytics Foundation

Add one consistent set of analytics used to present the same facts in the dashboard and daily summary.

**The admin will see/use:**

- Consistent metric definitions and time periods
- Matching dashboard and email results
- A unified view of trends, health, growth, and alerts
